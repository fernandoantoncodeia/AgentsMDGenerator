---
title: Short and Imperative
trigger: default-on
---

AGENTS.md is for AI agents, not documentation for a person. After any edit, re-read the whole file and cut anything verbose, redundant, or non-actionable.

- Prefer imperatives: "do X" not "consider doing X."
- Avoid prose paragraphs when a bulleted rule suffices.
- Each rule is one or two sentences.
- Soft cap 512 lines; hard cap 32 KiB.
